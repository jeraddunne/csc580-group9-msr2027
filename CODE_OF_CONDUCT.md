# Code of Conduct

Group 9 commits to a respectful, honest, and inclusive working environment.

- Treat every member as a peer. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask before concluding. Written text loses tone.
- Give feedback in pull requests that is specific, kind, and actionable.
- Credit work accurately. Never claim work you did not do; never hide help you received.
- Follow the university academic integrity policy and the course AI-use policy (see TEAM_CHARTER.md and ai-use-log.md).
- Respect the privacy of people in the datasets: do not attempt to deanonymise author codes, and do not contact repository owners.
- Raise concerns early. First to the Scrum Master of the sprint, then to the whole team, then to the instructor.

Violations are handled through the escalation path in TEAM_CHARTER.md section 9.
