## 3. How choices become assignments

At sprint planning the Product Owner and Scrum Master apply these rules in order and record the result in section 7.

1. Every workstream active in the sprint gets one lead and at least one support.
2. First choices are honoured where possible.
3. Each member holds at least one research item (W1, W4, W5) and one engineering item (W2, W3, W6, W7) per sprint (charter section 8).
4. Nobody leads more than two workstreams in the same sprint.
5. If a member's contribution share was outside 15% to 45% last sprint (metrics dashboard), rebalance toward them or away from them.
6. Tie-break: the member who received a lower choice last sprint goes first. Otherwise the Product Owner decides and the decision is logged in DECISION_LOG.md.

Across the semester, each member should lead at least one research workstream and one delivery workstream.

## 4. Claiming a specific issue

- Assign yourself on the issue, move it to **In progress**, and comment `Claiming, target <date>`.
- Hold at most two issues in progress at once (team WIP limit is five).
- If a claimed issue has no activity for 48 hours, the Scrum Master checks in. After 72 hours it returns to **Todo** unless you say otherwise.
- To swap work, comment on both issues and tag the other person.
- Stuck for more than a day? Open a Blocker issue.

## 5. Pairing

Pairing is encouraged, especially across strengths (for example a statistics-strong member with a writing-strong member on W4). List a pair in the matrix as `Lead + Support`. Both names go on the issue as assignees so the contribution is visible. Pairs cannot approve each other's pull requests for work they co-authored; a third member reviews.
