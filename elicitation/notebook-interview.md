## 2. Protocol

1. **Scope before asking.** Each question names the tiers it may be answered from. A GitSkills question uses scope `gitskills` (A1, A2, A4, A6, A9), so it cannot be answered from the SpecMine data dictionary or from the group's own notes. Group-process questions use `group` or `process`.
2. **Word for word, in order.** The notebook asks the prepared questions exactly as written in `elicitation/questions.yaml`. A question is never reworded to get a better answer. When an answer was incomplete, the team asked a **follow-up**, a new question with its own ID (`NB-Q05a`) that is recorded as such. Three follow-ups were added after the first pass.
3. **What the notebook returns.** For each question: the three best-matching passages (at most two per file), quoted verbatim with a locator `source ID · file · section or page · line`, and a mechanical status. "Match" means at least half of the question's terms occur in the passages. It does **not** mean the passages answer the question. The terms no passage contains are listed; they often point at what the sources do not establish.
4. **Verify.** For each answer the team (a) opened the cited source at the locator and confirmed the passage is there and is what the source says (the preprint passages were also checked against the original PDF page), (b) decided whether the passages actually answer the question, (c) looked for other sources that agree or disagree, and (d) for claims about the data, ran a read-only check on the sample (`V01` to `V18`). Results:
   - **Verified**: the answer is supported by the cited source and, where checked, by the data.
   - **Partly verified**: part of the question is answered and supported; the rest is recorded as unanswered or unsupported.
   - **Contradicted**: a source or the data says something different; recorded in the Verification field.
   - **Unverified**: the notebook cited nothing that bears on the question. The team investigated independently and says where.
5. **Interpret.** The team interpretation says what the answer means for P-01 and which `RESEARCH_SPEC.md` requirement it informs.
